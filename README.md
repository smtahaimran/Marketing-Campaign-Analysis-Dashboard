# Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard:

An end-to-end marketing analytics project built to analyze one year of multi-channel marketing performance, uncover key insights, and turn them into clear, actionable recommendations, visualized in Power BI and summarized in a client-ready slide deck.

## Project Overview:

This project analyzes marketing performance data from **January 2024 to January 2025**, covering 8 advertising channels and 20 campaigns. The goal was to identify which channels and campaigns delivered the best return on investment, uncover where budget was being wasted, and provide data-driven recommendations for improving future marketing spend. 
This project was built to demonstrate an end-to-end marketing analytics workflow; from raw data to a polished, interactive dashboard and a clear business narrative.
## Objective:

- Consolidate scattered marketing performance data into a single, interactive view
- Identify top and bottom-performing channels, campaigns, and audience segments
- Deliver clear, actionable recommendations to improve marketing ROI

## Dataset

- **Records:** 7,965 rows of daily marketing activity
- **Time period:** 13 months (Jan 2024 – Jan 2025)
- **Channels:** Google Search, Google Display, Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads, LinkedIn Ads, TikTok Ads, YouTube Ads, Email Marketing
- **Campaigns:** 20 individual campaigns across Awareness, Consideration, Conversion, and Retention types
- **Fields:** Date, Channel, Campaign, Age Group, Gender, Device, Country, Customer Segment, Impressions, Clicks, Spend, Conversions, Revenue, and derived KPIs (CTR, Conversion Rate, CPC, CPA, ROAS)

## Key Insights

- The account generated **$7.75 in revenue for every $1 spent** (7.75x ROAS)
- **Email Marketing** was the strongest channel, returning **32x** on ad spend
- **LinkedIn Ads** was the weakest channel, the only one with a negative return
- **Retargeting – Cart Abandoners** generated the highest total revenue ($4.3M+)
- **Returning customers** were significantly more profitable than new customers
- Performance was consistent across all six countries analyzed, with no underperforming market

## Recommendations

- Reallocate budget from underperforming channels (LinkedIn, Google Display) toward Email Marketing and retargeting campaigns
- Increase investment in retention and loyalty-focused campaigns
- Improve ad targeting/creative to address the steep impression-to-click drop-off
- Continue monitoring seasonal cost spikes (August–September) when planning future budgets
